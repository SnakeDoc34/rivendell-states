🔥 Streaks make the difference

How stars and mastery work

A clean learning answer earns 2 stars. Three fresh skills in a row add 4 stars; five add 10; ten add 25; fifteen add 40; twenty add 60. Each state mastered adds 12 stars. Region badges add 30, and the All-50 Championship adds 100.

Each state/skill counts once per streak. Streak bonuses pay once per day. Mastered skills earn one review star per skill per day. Clues and corrected answers are learning turns; try again later for mastery.

Your first three purchases stay inexpensive. Later prices and extra-copy requirements rise. Master five more states to open three more collection spaces, up to 36 pieces. Every extra item must be purchased.
